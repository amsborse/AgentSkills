# Editing and presentation

## Rhythm

- The cut follows the feeling. Pressure: 1.5-3 s holds, hard cuts, cuts landing on the beat.
  Release: 6-12 s holds, dissolves, cuts that land between beats.
- Cut on motion or on a sound. A dissolve is a passage of time or a change of state; do not use
  it to hide a weak shot.
- The turn gets the hardest edit in the film: a cut to silence, a held black, or a single shot
  that runs longer than anything before it.
- Never let the camera move in every shot of a sequence. Alternate movement and stillness.

## Transitions

| Kind | Use | Length |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cut | place change, energy | 0 |
| dissolve | time passing, softening | 1-2.5 s |
| match | two worlds rhyme (see cinematography) | 1-2 s |
| fade from black | opening, rebirth | 1-3 s |

A transition is never longer than half of either shot. Check a still from the middle of every
dissolve: two busy images over each other look muddy. Dissolve from a busy frame into a quiet one.

## Grade

- One grade per world, small steps between them. Urgency: cooler, compressed, slightly desaturated.
  Nature: neutral to warm, open shadows. Intimacy: warm skin and sun, soft contrast.
- Keep saturation natural; warmth comes from light temperature. Add fine grain over the whole film
  so generated and procedural shots sit together.
- Check the histogram per shot: shadows not crushed, highlights rolling off.

## Captions and title

- A serif or humanist typeface at a calm size, in the lower third or centred on negative space,
  never over a face. At least 24 px from the edges at 1080p.
- Captions fade in over 0.6-0.8 s, hold for their reading time, and fade out before the next cut.
- The title, if any, comes over the final image after the music resolves.

## Export

- H.264, yuv420p, CRF 16-20, `+faststart`, AAC 48 kHz; master loudness about -14 LUFS, true peak
  at or below -1 dBTP.
- Choose a poster frame that stands alone (usually the final image), and save it next to the film.
- Write the provenance file and the actual cost with the export.
