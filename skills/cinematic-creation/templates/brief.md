- **Audience and place:** who watches, where (feed, cinema, site hero), sound on or off.
- **Length and format:** <seconds>, <16:9 | 9:16 | 1:1>, <fps>.
- **The one feeling:** what the viewer leaves with, in five words or fewer.
- **The turn:** the single moment the film changes state, and roughly when.
- **Worlds:** 2-4 places or states, each with camera, light, palette, sound and rhythm.
- **Final image:** the shot that ends the film and could carry the title.
- **Must be true:** facts, names, products, places that cannot be invented.
- **Free to invent:** everything else.
- **Words:** captions, narration or none; the language.
- **Music:** feeling, instruments, original or licensed (name the licence).
- **Avoid:** looks, clichés and messages that are off-brief.
- **Backends allowed:** local renderers, local models, and any paid service with its approval and
  spending limit. "None approved" is a valid answer.
- **Done means:** the acceptance check (rubric scores, a person has listened, delivered files).
