Three rooms, one sky

Each room keeps its own job. The colour of the light is what they share.

Kitchen

Bright and neutral while you cook, then down to a warm counter glow once the plates are cleared.

Living room

Follows the window. When the sky turns amber, so do the lamps, a few minutes behind it.

Bedroom

No blue after ten. A low ember by the bed, and a slow sunrise in the half hour before the alarm.

Set once, then forget it

Nothing to schedule. Lantern reads sunset for where you live and does the rest.

  1. Plug in the bridgeIt finds the lamps already in the house.
  2. Name the roomsKitchen, bedroom, the stairs.
  3. Pick a bedtimeThe evening is paced back from it.
  4. Leave it aloneA switch still works like a switch.
