# Response Style & Status Line

- Keep responses to 1-2 lines by default. Be direct and concise unless the user explicitly asks for more detail.
- For multi-step tasks, optionally update the status bar ETA & next task via `node ~/.gemini/config/scripts/agy-eta.js plan <steps> <minutes> "<next task>"`, `step "<next task>"`, and `done` (skip for quick answers).
