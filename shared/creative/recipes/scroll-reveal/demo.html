Recipe · Scroll progress and reveal

The page keeps pace with the reader.

The line at the top tracks reading progress. Sections arrive as they enter the viewport. Add ?fallback=1 to run the script path instead of native scroll timelines.

01

Driven by scroll position, not by time

Scroll back up and the motion reverses with you. There is no timer to fall out of step with.

02

Off the main thread

Native scroll timelines run in the compositor, so a busy page does not stutter them.

03

Visible unless proven otherwise

Nothing is hidden until the mechanism that reveals it is known to be running.

04

Quiet when asked

With reduced motion, or on the static tier, every section is simply there.

05

Lighter on small devices

The low tier keeps the fade and drops the travel.
