Recipe · Spring states

Feedback with weight.

Press, toggle and disclose. Each one is a damped spring written as a CSS linear() easing: no script runs while it moves.

Press

Sinks under the pointer, springs back on release.

Toggle

A native checkbox, so Space and forms just work.

Disclose

Through grid-template-rows, from 0fr to 1fr. Nothing is measured, and closed content leaves the tab order.
